Quick Reference

Common temperature benchmarks

Description Celsius (°C) Fahrenheit (°F) Kelvin (K)
Absolute Zero -273.15 -459.67 0
Freezing Point of Water 0 32 273.15
Room Temperature 22 71.6 295.15
Human Body Temperature 37 98.6 310.15
Boiling Point of Water 100 212 373.15

Celsius → Fahrenheit

°F = (°C × 9/5) + 32

Fahrenheit → Celsius

°C = (°F − 32) × 5/9

Celsius → Kelvin

K = °C + 273.15

Kelvin → Celsius

°C = K − 273.15

Fahrenheit → Kelvin

K = (°F − 32) × 5/9 + 273.15

Kelvin → Fahrenheit

°F = (K − 273.15) × 9/5 + 32
